# Position versus CDM

Adopt the useful half of CDM/Rosetta: common semantics and deterministic lifecycle functions. Reject the universal single valuation engine and firm-wide chain/ledger monoculture. Reconciliation is reduced by agreeing on meaning, inputs, observations, and lifecycle functions—not by forcing every consumer through one pricing runtime.

Keep the model firm-specific and product-family-scoped. Grow it from the traded book; do not model every OTC derivative up front.
