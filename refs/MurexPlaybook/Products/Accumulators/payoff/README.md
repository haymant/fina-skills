# Accumulator payoff chapters

These chapters normalize the economic payoff of the accumulator family into explicit state, cashflow, and delivery branches. They are implementation-oriented interpretations of the adjacent Murex product notes; they do not replace an authoritative Murex payoff script.

- [AQDQ](aqdq.md)

Each chapter separates documented behavior from implementation recommendations and distinguishes funding/principal-like exchanges, coupon or fee components, and embedded optionality.
